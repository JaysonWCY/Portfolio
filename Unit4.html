Security Frameworks

Q1. Which of the frameworks do you think would be applicable to the following organisations:

a. International bank

b. Large hospital

c. Large food manufacturing factory

Q2. Summarise the tests and recommendations you would make to the owners/ managers for each of the above businesses to help them use the frameworks and comply with industry standards.


The main frameworks mentioned were HIPAA, PCI DSS, Sarbanes-Oxley Act, ISO 27000 series, NIST Special Publication (SP) series, COBIT, CIS Controls, HITRUST Common Security Framework, COSO, FISMA, NERC CIP, Graham-Leach-Bliley Act, and GDPR. Also stated were the discussions about each of their formation, governing bodies, as well as general use cases.

International Bank:
An international bank would have to follow multiple of these guidelines as they handle a lot of personal and sensitive information. Some of these would be:

  • PCI DSS: for handling credit and debit card transactions.
  • GDPR: as banks handle private information from their customers.
  • ISO 27000 series and NIST Cybersecurity Framework: to ensure a strong cybersecurity system.
  • Legal regulated frameworks in any country they operate in (e.g. GLBA, FISMA).

Large Hospital:
A large hospital handles many sensitive patient information, such as their blood work, health details, family details, among other data.

  • HIPAA: if operating in the U.S., which dictates practices needed for protecting health information.
  • GDPR: if operating in the EU, for data protection and privacy.
  • ISO 27000 series: as a general cybersecurity practice.

Large Food Manufacturing Factory:
These factories would have to comply with local regulations. While most countries have no digital-process-specific frameworks for food manufacturing:

  • ISO 27000 series: remains useful for protecting internal systems and sensitive data.
  • NIST Cybersecurity Framework: to apply best practices in cybersecurity risk management.

Most frameworks like ISO 27000 and NIST CSF are general-purpose and represent industry best practices. Others, like HIPAA or GDPR, are more region or industry-specific and mandatory depending on the organisation’s location and operations.


Tests and Recommendations


In terms of tests, conducting audits and process evaluations against the desired frameworks is the general method for assessing compliance.

  • Assess the current compliance level (what is already being done correctly).
  • Identify gaps or non-compliant areas.
  • Apply remediation (implement missing policies, controls, training, etc.).

This process should ideally be phased and carried out over time to minimise disruption to operations and give staff time to adapt to new systems or workflows.
