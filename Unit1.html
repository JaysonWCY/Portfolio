Security Standards, Frameworks and Disaster Recovery

Based on your reading of the case study listed above (Kovaitė and Stankevičienė, 2019), answer the following questions in the discussion forum:

▪ What do the authors mean by the term ‘Industry 4.0’ - give two examples.

▪ Give two real-world examples of risks that fit into the authors categories.

▪ Find another journal article that either supports or contradicts the points made in the cited study.


In the context of the case study, Industry 4.0 is the process of digitalisation that manufacturing and industrial enterprises go through by the integration of advancing technology. It is compared to industrial revolution and is regarding the focus on autonomous systems piloted by machine learning.

In the case study, some examples were the usage of AI to reduce the requirement of complex human thinking as well as robotics to reduce the usage of physical human labour.

In terms of data security risk, on 12 May 2017, WannaCry ransomware was used to attack computers using Microsoft Windows operating systems, which held important data, to hold such data for ransom. This spread was only possible due to how the systems were able to infect each other.

For financial risk, General Electric (GE) had placed a huge sum of money into their new platform-as-a-service, Predix, which operates regarding IoT to connect devices and provide analytics. However, they aimed too big and wanted to create a lot of their own solutions, which included creating their own data center, among other modules. This caused them to run out of money and the whole project to fail.

An article by Budagov and Sukhova (2020) supports the points made by Kovaitė and Stankevičienė by giving a more in-depth analysis about GE’s Predix platform failure.


References
