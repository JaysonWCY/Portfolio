Software Quality Monitoring in Python

Reflection on Software Quality. Review the article by Verdugo et al (2024). Select a timelier academic article on software quality. Discuss, in 300 words, the major differences in relation to software quality between the two articles.

RESPONSE

The article explores standardized approaches to evaluating software quality, particularly through the use of ISO and IEC frameworks. With a data set spanning 25 years, the research offers reliable foundation for its findings. It places strong emphasis on key quality attributes such as maintainability and functional suitability, both of which are crucial in software development.

However, the study leans heavily on retrospective practices and offers limited insight into future developments in the field. Additionally, its focus on an accredited laboratory setting may not translate well to more flexible, agile environments. The article also falls short in addressing emerging trends and modern technologies shaping today’s software landscape.

The article by Giamattei et al. (2024) focuses on newer technologies and emphasizes identifying the root causes of software quality issues. This approach is particularly relevant for modern architectures, such as distributed systems, as it enables more targeted and effective quality assurance strategies.

However, the study is primarily a systematic review and does not provide concrete evidence through technical implementation or real-world case studies. Additionally, the use of causal reasoning techniques requires specialized knowledge and training, making it less accessible to general practitioners. As a result, this approach is not yet widely adopted or recognized in mainstream software quality practices.

References:

  • Giamattei, L., Guerriero, A., Pietrantuono, R., & Russo, S. (2025). Causal reasoning in Software Quality Assurance: A systematic review. Information and Software Technology, 178, 107599. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.infsof.2024.107599
  • Verdugo, J., Oviedo, J., Rodriguez, M., & Piattini, M. (2024). Connecting Research and Practice for Software Product Quality Evaluation and Certification A Software Laboratory’s 25-Year Journey. IEEE Software, 41(3), 33–40. https://doi.org/10.1109/MS.2024.3357119
